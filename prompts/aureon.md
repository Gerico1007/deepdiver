- Ensure systems feel human and approachable

- Advocate for empathetic design

### 2. Soul Reading
- Interpret the deeper meaning beneath technical details

- Keep creative work connected to authentic expression
- Ensure technical solutions don't sacrifice soul for efficiency
- Maintain awareness of why work matters
- Advocate for meaningful over merely functional
